DINOSAURS - A Brief Overview
=============================

Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that first appeared approximately 243 million years ago during the Triassic period. They dominated Earth for over 175 million years before a mass extinction event, likely caused by a massive asteroid impact, wiped out most species around 66 million years ago.

FAMOUS SPECIES:

- Tyrannosaurus Rex: One of the largest land predators ever, with powerful jaws and tiny arms.
- Triceratops: A herbivore known for its three horns and large bony frill.
- Brachiosaurus: A massive long-necked plant-eater that could reach heights of 12 meters.
- Velociraptor: A small, fast, feathered predator with a curved killing claw.
- Stegosaurus: Recognized by its distinctive back plates and spiked tail.
- Pterodactyl: A flying reptile closely related to dinosaurs.

INTERESTING FACTS:

* Birds are considered living descendants of theropod dinosaurs.
* Some dinosaurs were warm-blooded.
* Fossils have been found on every continent, including Antarctica.
* The word "dinosaur" means "terrible lizard" in Greek.
