Dinosaurs

A little arcade of prehistoric games.

Pick a game below. Everything runs entirely in your browser — no installs, no accounts.

🎩

DinoHat

Multiplayer hat game: fill the hat with catchphrases, then race the clock to guess who said what.

 🦕

DinoMemory

Classic memory match with 16 dino-themed cards. Fewer moves and less time means a better score.

 🦖

DinoSnake

Snake, but the head is a dinosaur. Chomp eggs, grow longer, don't hit the walls or your own tail.

 🥚

DinoWordle

Wordle with a prehistoric twist. Guess the 5-letter dino-world word in 6 tries.

Other dino stuff in this repo

  • TRex.java — an ASCII T-Rex that runs back and forth in your terminal.
  • DinoWine.java — a Java program.
  • dinosaurs.txt — quick facts about famous dinosaur species.
  • trex-ascii.txt — a standalone ASCII T-Rex.
